A Tangency Foundation worksheet. Score every stakeholder on two axes (1 = low, 5 = high), plot them on the grid, then assign an engagement tactic per quadrant.

- **Impacted by Issue (Impact):** How directly does this issue touch them?
- **Can Impact Issue (Influence):** How much power do they have to accelerate or block change?

## Step 2 — The Grid

```
 Influence
    5 |  Q4: DECISION-MAKERS /        |  Q1: CAMPAIGN CORE
      |      VALIDATORS               |
      |  Tight briefs, tailored asks  |  Co-design strategy
    3 |-------------------------------+-------------------------------
      |  Q3: KEEP INFORMED            |  Q2: NARRATIVE DRIVERS
      |  Updates; activate if         |  Build capacity; center
    1 |  their stake grows            |  their stories
      +-------------------------------+------------------------------- Impact
      1                               3                               5
```

- **Q1 — High Impact / High Influence:** Co-design strategy with them. They are your campaign core.
- **Q2 — High Impact / Low Influence:** Build capacity so their stories drive the narrative.
- **Q3 — Low Impact / Low Influence:** Keep informed; activate if they become more affected.
- **Q4 — Low Impact / High Influence:** Treat as decision-makers or validators. Prepare tight briefs and asks.
