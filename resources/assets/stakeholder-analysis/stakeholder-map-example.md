# Stakeholder Map — Filled Example: "Clean Air Now"

**Project / Campaign:** Clean Air Now — clean-trucking ordinance
**Date:** March 2025  **Facilitator:** Student organizing team

Student organizers want their city to pass a clean-trucking ordinance limiting diesel truck traffic near homes and schools by the freeway corridor.

## Step 1 — Stakeholder Inventory

| Stakeholder | Type | Relationship to us | Impact (1–5) | Influence (1–5) | Quadrant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parents living near freeway | Individuals | Supportive, some active | 5 | 2 | Q2 |
| Youth with asthma (and peers) | Individuals | Core members | 5 | 3 | Q2 |
| Neighborhood association | Org | Friendly, cautious | 4 | 3 | Q1 |
| School nurses & pediatric clinic | Professionals | Sympathetic experts | 4 | 4 | Q1 |
| City Council president | Agency | Undecided | 2 | 5 | Q4 |
| Council transportation committee | Agency | Mixed | 2 | 5 | Q4 |
| Logistics & trucking lobby | Business | Opposed | 2 | 5 | Q4 |
| Local environmental nonprofit | Org | Ally, limited capacity | 3 | 4 | Q1 |
| Local newspaper health reporter | Media | Neutral, interested | 1 | 4 | Q4 |
| Residents elsewhere in city | Individuals | Mostly unaware | 2 | 2 | Q3 |

## Step 2 — How the Quadrants Drove Tactics

- **Q1 (Campaign core):** Neighborhood association, school nurses, environmental nonprofit → invited into a strategy table; media trainings so they can speak for the campaign.
- **Q2 (Narrative drivers):** Parents and youth scored 5 on impact but only 2–3 on influence → listening sessions to gather testimony, then storytelling and public-comment coaching so their experiences lead the narrative.
- **Q3 (Keep informed):** Residents citywide → monthly newsletter and social updates; activate if route changes touch their streets.
- **Q4 (Decision-makers / validators):** Council leaders and the logistics lobby have low direct impact but high power → prepared a health + economic brief, secured nurse and business-ally endorsements *before* requesting meetings; pitched the health reporter a data-plus-family story.
