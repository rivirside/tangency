# SWOT Analysis — Filled Example: Community Fridge Expansion

**Project / Organization:** Community Fridge crew
**Date:** February 2025  **Facilitator:** Volunteer coordinator
**Mission focus:** Free, dignified, 24/7 food access — growing from one fridge to three.

## The Quadrant

| **STRENGTHS** (internal, helpful) | **WEAKNESSES** (internal, harmful) |
|---|---|
| 1. Trusted, reliable volunteer base | 1. Limited cold-chain gear (coolers, backup power) |
| 2. Steady grocery store donation pipeline | 2. Volunteer burnout — same 6 people doing everything |
| 3. Engaged online supporter community | 3. No formal property agreements for fridge sites |

| **OPPORTUNITIES** (external, helpful) | **THREATS** (external, harmful) |
|---|---|
| 1. City micro-grant for food access projects | 1. Rising utility costs at host sites |
| 2. Culinary school seeking community partner | 2. Vandalism at unattended sites |
| 3. Local media interest in mutual aid | 3. Increased health department inspections |

## Action Translation

**Strength → Opportunity matches**

| Strength | Opportunity it unlocks | Action | Owner | By when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grocery donation pipeline (credibility) | City micro-grant | Apply, citing 12 months of donation logs — fund 2 new fridges | Grants lead | Mar 15 |
| Trusted volunteers + online community | Media interest | Pitch "day in the life of a fridge" story; recruit volunteers from coverage | Comms volunteer | Mar 30 |
| Volunteer base | Culinary school partnership | Students prep ready-to-eat meals — relieves volunteer fatigue (Weakness 2) | Coordinator | Apr 10 |

**Threat mitigations**

| Threat | Mitigation plan | Owner | By when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inspections | Draft MOUs with property owners defining cleaning schedule & responsibility | Site leads | Apr 1 |
| Vandalism | MOUs add host-site lighting/cameras; community "fridge guardians" rota | Site leads | Apr 1 |
| Rising utilities | Include 12 months of utility support per site in micro-grant budget | Grants lead | Mar 15 |

**Weakness to tackle first:** Volunteer burnout — culinary partnership + recruitment from media coverage.

- [x] Metrics defined — incidents per site per month; volunteer hours per person per week
